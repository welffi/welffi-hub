# welffi hub

a little hub of some vibecoded programs i've made to make my life a little easier

## programs

- **account manager**: log in to your roblox accounts and launch them together
- **[welffi animate](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-animate)**: a roblox r6 animator
- **macros**: coming later

## how to download

1. get `whub.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-hub/releases/latest) and run it
2. you need the [.net 10 desktop runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0) installed
3. click a program's tile on the home page. welffi animate gets downloaded for you, and the hub tells you when there's an update
