# welffi hub

a little hub of some vibecoded programs i've made to make my life a little easier

## programs

- **[welffi accounts](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-accounts)**: log in to your roblox accounts and launch them together
- **[welffi animate](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-animate)**: a roblox r6 animator
- **[welffi macros](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-macros)**: the ctd loop, plus a recorder for your own inputs
- **[welffi chest](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-chest)**: count your fortnite chests and track your odds, with a small overlay

## how to download

1. get `welffi-hub.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-hub/releases/latest) and run it. nothing else needs installing
2. click a program's tile. it gets downloaded for you, and the hub tells you when there's an update
3. the hub updates itself too. when a new version is out, click "update" at the top

if you used the account manager in the old hub, welffi accounts brings your accounts over the first time you open it
