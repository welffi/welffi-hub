# welffi hub

a little hub of some vibecoded programs i've made to make my life a little easier

## programs

- **welffi accounts**: log in to your roblox accounts and launch them together. moving into its own program soon
- **[welffi animate](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-animate)**: a roblox r6 animator
- **macros**: coming later

## how to download

1. get `welffi-hub.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-hub/releases/latest) and run it. nothing else needs installing
2. click a program's tile. it gets downloaded for you, and the hub tells you when there's an update
3. the hub updates itself too. when a new version is out, click "update" at the top

the old account manager is still in `whub.exe` from [v0.1.0](https://github.com/welffi/welffi-hub/releases/tag/v0.1.0) until welffi accounts is out. that one needs the [.net 10 desktop runtime](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/download/dotnet/10.0)
